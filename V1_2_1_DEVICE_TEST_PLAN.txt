GOVIND PERSONAL VAULT v1.2.1 DEVICE TEST PLAN

A. UPDATE SAFETY
1. Install v1.2.1 directly over v1.2.0; do not uninstall.
2. Confirm existing passwords, notes, media and documents remain available.

B. FULLSCREEN VIDEO
1. Open a 16:9 video and rotate to landscape.
2. Confirm the video fills the immersive canvas without a retained black strip at the bottom.
3. Tap once: controls appear. Wait while playing: controls auto-hide.
4. Cycle Resize through Zoom, Fill and Fit. Fit may intentionally show letterboxing.
5. Confirm Zoom is the automatic landscape default before manually selecting another mode.

C. GESTURE CONTROLS
1. On the LEFT half, swipe upward: brightness increases. Swipe downward: brightness decreases.
2. On the RIGHT half, swipe upward: media volume increases. Swipe downward: media volume decreases.
3. Confirm a percentage overlay appears and then disappears automatically.
4. Confirm a simple tap still toggles playback controls and does not change brightness/volume.
5. Confirm horizontal movement does not accidentally trigger vertical adjustment.
6. Confirm brightness change applies only to the Vault player window; global device brightness is not rewritten.

D. SECURITY / LIFECYCLE
1. Lock the screen during playback: the player closes and the vault locks.
2. Return to the app: authentication is required.
3. Confirm screenshots/screen recording remain blocked by FLAG_SECURE.
4. Confirm playback still decrypts on the fly and no plaintext media file appears in app cache/storage.
5. Test portrait-to-landscape and landscape-to-portrait several times during playback.

E. EDGE CASES
1. Test volume at 0% and 100%.
2. Test brightness near minimum and maximum.
3. Test a video with unusual aspect ratio and verify Resize choices.
4. Test pause, seek, audio-track switch, export and delete after gesture use.
