GOVIND PERSONAL VAULT 1.2.0 - DEVICE TEST PLAN

Install rule
- Install the signed v1.2.0 APK as an UPDATE over v1.1.0.
- Do not uninstall the existing app.
- Confirm old passwords, notes, and media still appear.

A. Dashboard / landscape
1. Open Passwords and rotate portrait -> landscape.
2. Swipe vertically from the center of the dashboard.
3. Confirm stats, four categories, search, all rows, empty state, and add button are reachable.
4. Repeat for Notes and with 20+ entries.
5. On a narrow portrait device, confirm stats/categories use a readable two-row layout.

B. Video player
1. Play a video in portrait, then rotate to landscape.
2. Confirm status/navigation bars and app title/metadata chrome hide.
3. Confirm the video expands after controls auto-hide (about 3.5 seconds while playing).
4. Tap video: controls appear. Wait: controls hide again.
5. Cycle Resize: Fit -> Zoom -> Fill -> Fit.
6. Pause: controls remain visible. Resume: they auto-hide.
7. Seek, change speed, and change audio track if available.
8. Rotate back to portrait and confirm normal title/actions return.
9. Turn screen off during playback and confirm vault locks/player stops.

C. Documents Vault
1. Open Documents from the dashboard.
2. Import PDF, TXT, DOC/DOCX, XLS/XLSX, PPT/PPTX, ZIP, and an unknown non-media file.
3. Confirm photo/video/audio selection is rejected and points to Media Vault.
4. Confirm document names/types/sizes appear and search works.
5. Open a document detail screen and export it.
6. Confirm the exported copy appears under Downloads/Govind Personal Vault and opens normally.
7. Confirm the encrypted vault copy remains after export.
8. Delete one encrypted document and confirm it disappears after relaunch.
9. Lock during import/export and confirm no incomplete visible output remains.
10. Test low-storage and corrupted/missing encrypted-file error messages when practical.

D. Regression
1. Import/play photos, videos, and audio in Media Vault.
2. Confirm Documents do not appear in Media filters/count.
3. Confirm old media exports still go to Pictures/Movies/Music.
4. Confirm biometric/PIN/recovery and auto-lock still work.
5. Confirm app has no Internet or broad storage permission in system settings.
